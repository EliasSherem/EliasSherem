Founder & CTO of a personal finance management platform.

- Financial analytics and interactive dashboards
- Investment portfolio management
- Recurring transactions and budgeting
- AI-powered receipt recognition
- Web and mobile applications
- Subscription-based SaaS architecture

**Stack:** React, TypeScript, Node.js, Supabase, Flutter, Stripe, Google Cloud.

### Industrial Metal 3D Printer Platform

Reverse-engineered and developed an independent industrial metal 3D printing control and build-preparation platform.

- PLC communication and hardware integration
- 3D model rendering and manipulation
- Support structure generation
- Model slicing and laser toolpath generation
- STL, CLI and CLI+ compatibility
- Hardware simulation and telemetry
- Native laser-scanner integration

**Stack:** Python, React, Modbus TCP, RTC4, PySLM, NumPy, Trimesh, Shapely, Ghidra, Wireshark.

### AI-Powered WhatsApp Commerce

End-to-end conversational commerce and business automation platform.

- Automated WhatsApp ordering
- Integrated e-commerce storefront
- Local AI inference and image recognition
- Customer and order management
- Business administration dashboard
- Automated customer notifications

**Stack:** n8n, Supabase, Ollama, WhatsApp Cloud API, Docker.
